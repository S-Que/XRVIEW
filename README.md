# XRVIEW
Extended Reality(XR) viewer is what this is.
This is a quickly put together 3D viewing, dimensional analysis and XR projection tool.
I have targeted STEP, STL, JSON, OBJ, 3Mf...for now.
I basically wanted something I can use on my phone or tablet, found something in the Google app store which was ok,
however littered with ads or I have to pay $50 to unlock and keep.
In this day and age such things require a rethink, besides "Ain't no body paying for that!"
So I engineered my own tool, for what I need it to do.
If I could charge I would sell the tool for $4.99 or €4.99 or less.

I may add a slicer function, but it isn't necessary. For now I am just running UI and UX trials.
Oh by the way, you can project your model or assembly unto a surface/ table, how nice is that?
